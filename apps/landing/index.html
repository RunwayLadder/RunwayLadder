Fixed income for onchain treasuries · Solana

Income a treasury can put in a budget.

Deposit stablecoins once and get a schedule of fixed amounts on fixed dates — set when you deposit, not a floating APY. When a date arrives, the money comes back to you or rolls to the far end of the schedule, without you signing again.

Where it runs today ↓

Local validator only · not on devnet yet · not audited.

2
When the source pays less

A shortfall is paid in one order, and you see it before it is paid.

  1. 1The protocol buffer — filled by fees and by any yield above the promise.
  2. 2If the buffer is empty, every rung of that date is cut by the same ratio.

Paid less than promised, unmarked, is not a state the program can be in: the rung becomes redeemed with deficit, and the dashboard shows the amount that will actually arrive before you redeem.

3
Who signs what

Three parties, and what each can and cannot do.

PartyCanCannot

Treasurer

Open the schedule in one signature. Redeem a matured rung. Turn rolling off at any time, without touching rungs already issued.

Be assumed to hold one key — the owner can be a multisig.

Keeper

Run by anyone. Settle matured dates and roll the rungs of schedules set to roll.

Send money anywhere: a roll has no destination parameter — the new rung lands in the same schedule, with the same owner.

Operator

Set the rate of each date. The rate, who set it and when are on screen before you sign.

Be a market price. This is a deliberate point of trust.

4
Terms

Terms you accept.

  1. 1No early exit. Money is locked until its date. There is no way to get it out sooner today.
  2. 2The rate comes from a set adapter, not from Kamino. The schedule cannot tell them apart; the live source is the next milestone.
  3. 3The operator sets the rate. The program enforces the promise, not that the promise was a fair price.
  4. 4Early dates are thinly protected. The buffer starts with fees only.
  5. 5Rent is not returned yet. Closing a finished schedule to get it back is planned, not built.
  6. 6Not audited. The demo never goes to mainnet.
  7. 7No multisig run yet. The design allows a Squads safe as owner; no run has used one.
